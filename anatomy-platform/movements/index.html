Elbow flexion and extension

The forearm bends toward the upper arm in flexion, then moves away in extension. The humerus remains fixed and the hand moves with the forearm.

Sequence and positions

  1. Start pose: extended source pose.
  2. Flexion: the forearm and hand bend toward the upper arm.
  3. Peak flexion: the illustrative endpoint.
  4. Extension: the forearm and hand return toward the starting pose.

The wrist and fingers stay fixed. Joint axis, direction, clearance and excursion require anatomical review.

Source and review notes

This source-referenced hinge uses the Z-Biomechanics RightForeArm control’s local Z axis and the ulna rig head as pivot, through an illustrative 0–120° excursion. This is not measured individual motion or a universal range of motion. The revised forearm study uses the source radial-head rig point and a transverse circle fit to ulna vertices 6–12 mm proximal to its distal tip. The original distal-tip centroid axis is retained for comparison. These are unreviewed geometric proxies. It holds the ulna fixed and rotates the radius and hand; coupled ulnar and wrist motion are omitted. Finger articulation is not included.
