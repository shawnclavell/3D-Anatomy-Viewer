Model credits and educational-use notice

This viewer is an instructional anatomy resource. It is not a diagnostic, surgical-planning, or patient-care tool, and it does not represent every anatomical variation.

Anatomy model sources

The skeleton, skull, exploded-skull, skull-base, upper-limb, and lower-limb files are browser-oriented adaptations of models from Z-Anatomy, the libre 3D atlas of anatomy.

Changes made for this viewer

The Z-Anatomy source models were modified for web-based teaching in 2026. Changes include bilateral mirroring where source geometry supplied one side, applied transforms and repaired normals, per-object materials for selection, focused regional derivatives, an exploded-skull arrangement, Draco web compression, camera presets, and instructor-reviewed teaching annotations. The muscular-system review preview combines the source muscular collections with the completed skeleton, omits organizational helper objects, applies complete mirrored transforms, evaluates source modifiers, recalculates normals, preserves source-assigned tendon regions, and assigns separate muscle, fascia, tendon, sheath, and bursa colors. Source origin/insertion surface meshes are shown as provisional selected-structure overlays. Anatomical alignment and attachment relationships remain under review.

Muscle information

Educational origin, insertion, action and innervation summaries were assembled for this viewer in 2026. Each muscle entry links to its anatomy reference. These summaries are separate from the provisional colored attachment-surface relationships. Regional subdivisions and anatomical variation are noted where relevant; instructor content review remains pending.

Viewer implementation

The nervous-system review derivative evaluates Z-Anatomy meshes and nerve curves with their source placement and laterality, converts them to independently selectable surfaces, and adds optional skeletal context. Display groups and colors are teaching aids pending instructor review. Labels, organizational helpers and curves without source surfaces are omitted. The derivative retains CC BY-SA 4.0 and the Z-Anatomy and BodyParts3D attribution above.
