Skin and body regions

Open the 3D preview

This model represents the external body surface of the source adult male atlas. It does not separate epidermis, dermis, or subcutaneous tissue. It retains 250 source patches; a named patch can include several small surface features.

Orientation and selection

Left and right always mean the subject’s left and right. In an anterior view, the subject’s right appears on your left. Anterior means toward the front, posterior toward the back, medial toward the midline, and lateral away from it. Proximal is nearer a limb’s attachment to the trunk; distal is farther from it.

Search by a source name or a draft course synonym, choose a result, then press Select structure. Isolate shows the selected patch; Ghost Others keeps the surrounding surface faintly visible. Show All exits isolation or ghosting. To reveal muscles under a surface patch, select the patch and use Structure actions, then Remove. Use Remove and restore structures to replace removed patches. Anatomy Layers can hide whole body areas. Views includes front, back, side, palms, and soles.

Regional relationships

Cephalic refers to the head, including cranial and facial areas. Cervical refers to the neck. The trunk includes anterior thoracic and abdominal surfaces, posterior back, and pelvic/perineal surfaces. Upper limbs include the shoulder, arm, elbow, forearm, wrist, and hand. Lower limbs include the hip, thigh, knee, leg, ankle, and foot.

Brachial means arm; antebrachial means forearm. Palmar refers to the palm. Femoral means thigh; patellar refers to the front of the knee and popliteal to its back. Crural refers here to the anterior leg and sural to the calf. Plantar refers to the sole; dorsal hand and foot surfaces face away from the palm and sole.

The lumbar back region and the lateral (lumbar) abdominal region are distinct locations. Abdominal regions include epigastric, hypochondriac, umbilical, lateral abdominal, inguinal (iliac), and hypogastric areas. The source divides several midline areas into left and right patches.

Review status

Source names are retained. Course synonyms below are draft teaching mappings, not newly drawn boundaries. Some source surfaces do not isolate individual digits. This guide and model need instructor review for learning-objective equivalence.
